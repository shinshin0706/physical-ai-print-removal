# Sources and redistribution

The submitted printer is an independently written primitive scene. Its internal boxes, parked heights and furniture are design assumptions, not an accurate replica of a Bambu A1. The 256 × 256 mm build area and A1 context reference the manufacturer's published specifications. No MakerWorld CAD, converted meshes, CAD-rendered images, or weights trained on those images are included.

- SO-101: Google DeepMind MuJoCo Menagerie / TheRobotStudio, pinned commit `a662de603224c47123b0783821c58ad4402ad3e3`, Apache License 2.0. The original license accompanies `vendor/robotstudio_so101/`. Printed parts are recolored white at runtime; original vendor assets are unchanged. A custom printed spatula is attached with an idealized rigid grasp. https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie
- ST3215 torque assumption: 12 V variant; 0.980665 Nm model cap. This does not establish measured sustained torque, grip force or peeling ability. https://docs.waveshare.com/ST3215_Servo
- A1: https://bambulab.com/en/a1/tech-specs and https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/a1/manual/faq
- Webcam reference: C920-class, 78-degree diagonal field of view, approximated as a distortion-free pinhole camera. No actual camera calibration is supplied. https://www.logi.com/en-eu/shop/p/c920-pro-hd-webcam.960-001055
- ArUco: OpenCV DICT_4X4_50, synthetic marker images generated by OpenCV. https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d5/dae/tutorial_aruco_detection.html
- Collection-bin external size reference: IKEA TROFAST 420 × 300 × 100 mm; wall and interior dimensions are assumptions. https://www.ikea.com/jp/ja/p/trofast-storage-box-light-green-70582090/

Earlier local design exploration used vajojajo's A1 STEP model (https://makerworld.com/en/models/770920-bambulab-a1-cad-step-model). Its displayed Standard Digital File License restricts redistribution of the digital object and derivatives. The public experiment was independently rebuilt using primitive geometry and retrained on new images, rather than packaging that model or its outputs. Prior CAD-based metrics are excluded from the reported public-scene comparison.

No company assets, course handout copies, names, affiliations, credentials, or local user paths belong in this package. Generated scene XML is not packaged; asset paths are resolved on the recipient machine by the included scene builder.
